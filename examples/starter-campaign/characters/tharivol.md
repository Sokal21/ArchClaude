# Tharivol

A half-elf paladin sworn to the Order of the Silver Hand. Former village guard from Millhaven, now questing to prove himself worthy of the Order's full investiture.

## Personality
Earnest, occasionally naive, driven by a deep sense of fairness. Tends to see the best in people — which has gotten him into trouble more than once.

## Hooks
- Owes a debt to his mentor, Sir Aldric, who died defending Millhaven from bandits.
- Carries Sir Aldric's shield — a battered thing with the Silver Hand emblem barely visible.
- Haunted by the memory of a bandit he let escape during the raid. Was it mercy or cowardice?

## Voice notes
Speaks with conviction but not arrogance. Pauses before important statements. Occasionally quotes Sir Aldric's sayings.
