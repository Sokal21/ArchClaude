# Miravel

A tiefling wizard from the Arcane Academy of Thornwall. Left the academy under ambiguous circumstances — officially on "field research," unofficially asked to leave after an experiment went wrong.

## Personality
Intellectual, sarcastic, genuinely kind underneath the sharp tongue. Obsessed with magical theory. Keeps meticulous notes in a leather journal.

## Hooks
- The failed experiment at Thornwall left a faint scar of silver light on her left palm. It glows when powerful magic is nearby.
- Searching for a specific tome: "Vardek's Principia Arcana," rumored to be in a collector's private library somewhere in Goldspire.
- Has a complicated relationship with her academy mentor, Professor Wen, who voted to have her expelled.

## Voice notes
Quick, clipped sentences when stressed. Eloquent when explaining magic. Uses metaphors from magical theory in everyday speech.
