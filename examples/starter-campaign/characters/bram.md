# Bram

A human rogue from the Docks Ward of Goldspire. Grew up running errands for the Dock Rats — not quite a thieves' guild, not quite legitimate. Left the life behind after his best friend was killed in a job gone wrong.

## Personality
Street-smart, observant, distrustful of authority. Loyal to his friends to a fault. Has a weakness for gambling and a surprising knowledge of flowers (his grandmother was a florist).

## Hooks
- The Dock Rats want him back. His old boss, "Fingers" Marek, has sent word that Bram "owes a debt."
- Carries a locket with a pressed flower — his grandmother's last gift.
- Witnessed something in the Docks Ward he wasn't supposed to see. He won't talk about it.

## Voice notes
Speaks in short, direct sentences. Uses Docks Ward slang. Drops his formal register when surprised or angry.
