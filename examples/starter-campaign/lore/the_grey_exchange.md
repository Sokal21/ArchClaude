# The Grey Exchange

A shadowy network of information brokers, relic hunters, and antiquarians. Not evil per se — they value knowledge and profit in equal measure. But they've been known to crack open tombs that were sealed for good reason.

## Methods
- Send scouts (like Corvus) to locate sites of interest
- Hire adventurers for the dangerous work
- Acquire artifacts and sell them to the highest bidder — or keep the most dangerous ones

## Motives in this campaign
The Grey Exchange has identified the Hollow Wood vaults as pre-human in origin. They want the contents. They don't know what's sealed inside — and they don't particularly care.

## Known members
- Corvus Ashford (scout, currently in Millhaven)
- "The Broker" (their contact in Goldspire, identity unknown)
