# Corvus Ashford

A traveling merchant who arrived in Millhaven three days before the party. Thin, well-dressed, with an oiled black beard and calculating eyes. Trades in "curiosities" — old maps, strange artifacts, dubious potions.

## Hooks
- He's actually a scout for the Grey Exchange, a network of information brokers. He's in Millhaven because of the cairns.
- Carries a map fragment showing something deep in the Hollow Wood, marked with a symbol that matches the cairn sigils.
- Will offer the party 50 gold to retrieve "a stone tablet" from a ruin in the Wood. He knows more than he's saying about what's there.

## The secret
Corvus knows the cairns are markers for a buried vault from a pre-human civilization. The Grey Exchange wants what's inside. He does NOT know the vault is guarded.

## Voice notes
Smooth, measured, always sounds like he's appraising you. Offers compliments that feel like transactions. Never raises his voice.
