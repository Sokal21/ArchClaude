# Elara Thornwick

The innkeeper of The Sleeping Fox in Millhaven. A broad-shouldered woman in her fifties with silver-streaked red hair and a no-nonsense manner. Former adventurer — retired after losing two fingers on her left hand to a mimic.

## Hooks
- Knows more about the Hollow Wood than she lets on. Her adventuring party explored its edges twenty years ago.
- Has been receiving anonymous threatening letters telling her to "stop asking questions about the cairns."
- Keeps a locked chest under the bar. Won't say what's in it.

## Voice notes
Warm but guarded. Speaks plainly. Calls everyone "love" or "dear." Voice drops to a whisper when discussing the Hollow Wood.
