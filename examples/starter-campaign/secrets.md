# DM Secrets

<!-- These must NEVER leak into player-facing narration. -->

## The Vault Guardian
The spectral guardian in the Vault is the spirit of Kael Dawnshield, a paladin who gave his life to seal the vaults 800 years ago. If the party can learn his name (from Brother Aldous at the chapel, or from carvings in Chamber 2), they can address him directly and he will be more willing to talk.

## Corvus's True Mission
Corvus is not just buying a tablet. The Grey Exchange has already opened one vault in the Drakefall Mountains and found something that terrified them. They believe the Hollow Wood vaults contain a countermeasure. Corvus doesn't know the details — he just knows the Exchange is unusually desperate.

## Elara's Past
Elara's adventuring party didn't just explore the edges of the Hollow Wood. They entered the Vault 20 years ago and fled when they woke... something. She lost her fingers to it. The thing went back to sleep, but she's been watching the cairns ever since. The threatening letters are from the Grey Exchange, warning her to stay out of their way.

## The Bigger Picture
The vaults contain remnants of a pre-human civilization that sealed away a slumbering entity beneath the hills. Each vault is a lock. Opening them weakens the seal. The Grey Exchange has already opened one. Two more remain, including this one.
