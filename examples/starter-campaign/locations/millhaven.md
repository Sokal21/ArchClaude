# Millhaven

A small market town on the edge of the Hollow Wood. Population ~800. Known for its flour mills (hence the name) and as the last supply stop before the Wood's interior.

## Notable features
- **The Sleeping Fox** — The town's main inn, run by Elara Thornwick. Two stories, a decent kitchen, six rooms.
- **The Mill Square** — Central marketplace. Busy in the mornings, quiet by dusk.
- **The Chapel of the Morning** — Small shrine to Lathander, tended by Brother Aldous (elderly, mostly deaf, very kind).
- **The Stockade** — Town watch headquarters. Captain Maren Holt runs a tight ship with twelve guards.

## Atmosphere
Sleepy and provincial. The kind of place where strangers get noticed. Recent events — missing livestock, strange sounds from the Wood at night — have put everyone on edge.
