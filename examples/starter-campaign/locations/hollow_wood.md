# The Hollow Wood

An ancient forest stretching 40 miles north of Millhaven. Dense canopy, minimal undergrowth in the deep interior. Named for the unsettling silence — birdsong stops about a mile in.

## Notable features
- **The Old Road** — A crumbling stone road running north through the Wood. Pre-dates the town by centuries. Locals avoid it.
- **The Cairns** — Stone markers scattered through the forest. Some are ancient (moss-covered, sunken). Others look recently disturbed. All bear the same spiral sigil.
- **The Vault** (unknown to players) — Buried beneath the largest cairn, 3 miles along the Old Road. A pre-human structure of black stone. Sealed.

## Atmosphere
Oppressively quiet past the first mile. Light filters strangely through the canopy — greener than it should be. The air smells of old stone and damp earth. At night, faint lights drift between the trees (will-o'-wisps).

## Encounters
- Outer wood: wolves, giant spiders, the occasional owlbear
- Deep wood: shadow-touched creatures, will-o'-wisps near the cairns
- The Vault approach: trapped, with a guardian
