# The Vault

A pre-human underground structure sealed beneath the largest cairn in the Hollow Wood. Built from black stone that absorbs light. Three chambers.

## Chamber 1: The Antechamber
- Stone doors, unsealed (Corvus's map shows how to open them)
- Walls covered in spiral carvings that glow faintly blue
- Trapped: pressure plates trigger darts from the walls (DC 13 DEX save, 2d6 piercing)

## Chamber 2: The Hall of Echoes
- A long hall with pillars. Sound echoes strangely — whispers from the far end.
- Contains the stone tablet Corvus wants. It's a map to other vaults.
- Two shadow creatures lurk here (reskinned Shadows from SRD, CR 1/2)

## Chamber 3: The Guardian's Rest
- Boss fight location. A Spectral Guardian (reskin of Specter, CR 1, boosted to HP 30)
- The guardian was a protector of this vault in life. Can be spoken to if the party tries diplomacy (DC 15 Persuasion).
- If negotiated with successfully, it reveals: "More vaults lie beneath the hills. What sleeps there must not wake."

## The twist
The tablet Corvus wants isn't just a map — it's a key. The Grey Exchange plans to open the other vaults. Whatever is sealed in them was sealed for a reason. This sets up the campaign's larger arc.
